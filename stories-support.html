Pillowby Support

Pillowby writes an illustrated bedtime story in chapters from your child's own idea, typed or said out loud. Can't find an answer here? Email twistedsocietyab@gmail.com. We read every message.

Making stories

How do I make a story?

On the home screen, type or say an idea and tap Make my story. The story friend writes it while you watch, and the book opens at chapter 1 as soon as that chapter is ready. The other chapters and the painted cover arrive while you read.

A story stopped halfway.

Open it from the shelf and tap Finish the story. Only the missing chapters are written, and finishing a story never counts toward the month's new stories.

Pillowby said "Let's try another idea".

Every idea is checked before a story is written, and some ideas aren't gentle enough for bedtime. Try another one, or pick one of the ideas on the home screen.

A story wasn't right.

Press and hold the book on the shelf, or open Text and page in the reader, and choose Report this story. The report sends us the story and the idea it came from, and you can delete the story at the same time. AI can make mistakes, so we suggest a grown-up stays nearby, especially with younger children.

Does Pillowby read stories aloud?

Not in this version. Stories are read together from the page.

How do I change the age, the name or the language?

Tap the Grown-ups button on the home screen and answer the quick grown-ups check. There you can change the child's age, first name, what they love, the story friend, the app's and the stories' language, and the bedtime look and reminder.

The microphone doesn't work.

In Grown-ups, under Spoken ideas, check that the microphone and speech recognition are allowed. If they were turned off, Open Settings takes you to the iPhone's settings for Pillowby.

The subscription

What does it include?

Up to 30 new stories a month, each with a painted cover. The month starts on the 1st, or on the day you subscribed, and each iPhone or iPad counts its own stories. Reading and rereading the shelf, and finishing a story that stopped, are always open without a subscription. You can choose a monthly or a yearly plan; the app and the App Store show the price in your currency before you buy.

How does the trial work?

New subscribers get a one-week trial. If notifications are on, Pillowby reminds you before it ends. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pillowby. Or in Pillowby, open Grown-ups, then Subscription, then Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

I subscribed, but Pillowby asks me to subscribe again.

In Grown-ups, under Subscription, tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with. If the purchase is waiting for a parent's approval (Ask to Buy), the subscription starts once it's approved.

I have a code.

In Grown-ups, under Subscription, tap Redeem a code.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your stories and your data

Where are our stories kept?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes the stories too. To delete some, press and hold a book on the shelf and choose Delete story, or use Grown-ups, then Stories.

What is sent to make a story?

The idea, the age you picked and the language go through our server to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the story. If you added them, so do the first name, girl or boy, what your child loves, the line about who is close to them and your last five titles. The cover is painted by OpenAI from a short picture description, without your child's words or name. Our server keeps no copy. Grown-ups, then How stories are made, shows it in the app, and Stop sending ideas turns it off until you agree again. The details are in our privacy policy.

How do I ask you to delete a report or a note I sent?

Email us with roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we'll delete it.
