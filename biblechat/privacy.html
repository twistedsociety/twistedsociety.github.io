Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Short version: your conversations, memories and what BibleChat learns about you stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. We send your messages to our AI provider only to answer them, and only after you agree. We don't sell data, don't show ads and don't track you across apps.

Who we are

BibleChat is made by Twisted Society AB, a company registered in Sweden ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: twistedsociety@mzr.se.

What we process, why, and on what basis

Your account

When you first open the app we create an anonymous account with a random identifier, so we can apply your plan, daily message limits and gifts. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we also receive the name and email address Apple shares (which may be a private relay address). Basis: performing our contract with you.

Messages you send to the AI

When you chat or use other AI features (daily brief, journeys, guided prayer, passage quotes, photo questions), the app sends your message, the recent conversation and the context needed for a good answer to our servers, which pass it to our AI provider, Anthropic, and stream the answer back. That context can include your language, preferred Bible wording, tradition, first name if you gave one, the memories most relevant to the message, and the answer style and interests BibleChat has learned. Our servers do not store your conversations or anything the AI writes for you. Anthropic does not use this data to train models and, under its API data policy, does not keep it after answering, except content its systems flag for safety, which it may keep for up to two years for trust and safety purposes.

Because your messages can reveal your religious beliefs, which is a special category of personal data, we ask for your explicit consent before your first AI message (GDPR Article 9(2)(a), and equivalent rules elsewhere). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Privacy and data; AI features then stay off.

What BibleChat learns about you

To make BibleChat personal, the app keeps a small profile on your device: topics you care about, the tone and length of answers you prefer, the times you usually open the app and the guides you like. It shapes your greeting, daily brief, suggestions, reminder time and how the AI answers. It is calculated and stored only on your devices (and your iCloud, if sync is on); only the parts a request needs are sent with that request. See and erase it in Settings > What BibleChat has learned.

Memories

If you turn on "Remember what I share", the AI suggests short facts to remember (for example, a prayer request or your church). They are stored only on your devices, you can see, edit and delete them in Settings, and you can turn the feature off. In the EU and some other markets it is off until you turn it on.

Safety checks

Every message is checked automatically for signs of crisis, abuse or danger so the app can show local helplines (see our crisis protocol). For the most serious flags we keep a short review record (the kind of risk, its severity, the language and a one-sentence reason) without your account identifier and without your words, to improve our safety protections. We keep anonymous counts of how often crisis resources are shown, as some laws require.

Your content on your devices

Conversations, memories, journeys and your progress, guide history, daily briefs and the passages you opened are stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud terms apply). We cannot read them. Turn on "Keep my data on this device only" to stop iCloud sync.

Shared conversations

If you choose to share a conversation as a link, we store a copy of that conversation on our servers so anyone with the link can read it and continue it in their own app. A shared link is public until you remove it. You can remove it in the app at any time, and all your shared links are deleted with your account. Only share what you are comfortable with others reading.

Invites (discontinued)

BibleChat no longer has invites or invite rewards. If you used them before 25 November 2026, we may still hold your old invite code, the accounts it connected, the reward days and an Apple push token from that time. We no longer use them, and they are deleted with your account.

Purchases

Apple handles payment. We receive the App Store transaction information (product, dates, renewal status) to give you Premium on your devices and to apply gifts. We never see your card details. Basis: contract, and our legal obligation to keep accounting records.

Usage statistics

If you allow it, the app sends anonymous product events (for example "onboarding finished" or "paywall shown") to PostHog, hosted in the EU, so we can improve the app. These events never contain your messages, prayers, tradition or faith details. In markets that require consent it is off until you turn it on; everywhere else you can turn it off in Settings. Basis: consent or our legitimate interest in improving the app.

Device and security data

We use Apple's App Attest to check that requests come from the genuine app, which protects the service from abuse. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Reports and support

If you report an answer, we receive the answer and the message you reported and keep them for 90 days to review them. If you email us, we receive your message and email address.

No Bible or content hosting

We don't host Bible texts or any other content library. Verses in answers are quoted by the AI.

Who we share data with

Only service providers who process data for us under data-processing terms:

  • Anthropic (United States): AI answers.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase: our servers, accounts and database.
  • PostHog (EU): usage statistics, if allowed.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, iCloud sync and notifications.

Where data leaves the EU/EEA, we rely on the European Commission's adequacy decisions (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or Standard Contractual Clauses. We don't sell personal data, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't use tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

How long we keep data

  • Account, plan and gift data, and old invite records: until you delete your account.
  • Shared conversations: until you remove the link or delete your account.
  • AI requests: not stored by us; see above for Anthropic.
  • Reported answers: 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: up to 24 months.
  • Purchase records: as long as accounting law requires (in Sweden, seven years).

Age and young people

BibleChat is for people 13 and older (14 to 16 in some countries, and 18 in India; the app shows the age for yours). The app asks which age group you are in when you set it up; it does not ask for or store your birth date. It is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If we learn that we have, we delete it; parents and guardians can write to us.

If you tell the app you are under 18, only that fact ("teenager") is sent with your messages so the AI can answer with extra care. For teenagers, product analytics is off unless they turn it on, conversations can't be published as public links, and the app does not show discount offers when the subscription screen is closed. Everything else on this page applies in the same way. If you are under 18, please read this page with a parent or guardian.

We follow the child-privacy rules that apply to us, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): BibleChat is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. In the EU and UK, the app is offered only from the age at which a young person can agree to their own data use in their country.

Your rights

You can access and download your data (Settings > Privacy and data > Export my data), correct it, delete it (Settings > Privacy and data > Delete my account, or see how to delete your account), object to processing, withdraw consent at any time, and ask for data portability. If you are in the EU/EEA you may complain to your data protection authority; in Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY). Residents of California and other places with similar laws have equivalent rights; we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. The app can be locked with Face ID or your passcode, and discreet mode hides content from notifications and the app switcher.

Changes

We will post changes here and tell you in the app when they are significant.
