Subscription Terms

Last updated 16 November 2026

Premium plans

BibleChat Premium is offered as auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly and yearly) and as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your currency before you buy and can differ by country. Premium includes unlimited chat, deep study, photo questions, unlimited guided sessions and every journey.

Payment and renewal

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price and length unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Manage or cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or in BibleChat under Settings > Subscription > Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

Free trials and introductory offers

Some plans include a free trial or introductory price, shown in the app before you buy. When a free trial ends, the subscription starts and you are charged unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy another subscription in the same group. Offers are limited to eligible customers, once per subscription group.

Special offers

We sometimes offer discounted prices (for example, when you leave the paywall, after a cancelled trial, for returning subscribers, during seasons such as Lent or Advent, or through offer codes). The discounted price, how long it lasts and the regular price after it are always shown before you confirm.

Lifetime

Lifetime is a single payment for Premium for as long as BibleChat Premium is offered, and doesn't renew.

Gifts

A gift is a one-time purchase of one year of Premium for someone else. You receive a code and a link to send them. Gifts don't renew. The recipient must redeem the code by the date shown; once redeemed, the year starts. If the recipient already has a subscription, it continues as normal and is not refunded.

Invite rewards

When a friend joins with your invite, you both get free days of Premium, as shown in the app. Reward days don't renew, can't be exchanged for money, are limited per person, and don't change or pause an App Store subscription you already have.

Support the mission

"Support the mission" is a one-time voluntary contribution. It doesn't change your plan or unlock features.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting the app or your BibleChat account does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

On a new device, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases.

Price changes

If a subscription price goes up, Apple tells you in advance and, where required, asks for your consent before renewing at the new price.
