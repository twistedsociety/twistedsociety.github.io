Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open BibleChat and tap your profile button.
  2. Go to Settings > Privacy and data.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, your shared conversation links, your gift records, any old invite records and everything else our servers hold about you, and erases your conversations, memories, journeys, guide history and what BibleChat has learned from the device and your iCloud. It can't be undone.

Without the app

Write to twistedsociety@mzr.se from the email address linked to your Sign in with Apple account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it first in your iPhone's Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
