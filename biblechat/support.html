Subscriptions and purchases

How do I cancel?

Open your iPhone's Settings > your name > Subscriptions > BibleChat, or in BibleChat go to Settings > Subscription > Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal (or before a free trial ends) to avoid being charged.

I paid but don't have Premium.

Go to Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with. If a purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), Premium starts once it's approved.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do gifts work?

In Settings > Subscription > Gift a year of Premium, buy a gift and send the code or link. The recipient taps the link or enters the code in Settings > Subscription > Redeem a gift.

How do invites work?

In Settings > Invite friends, share your invite link. When a friend joins with it, you both get free days of Premium. A friend who already has the app can't use an invite; a new friend can also enter your code when setting up BibleChat.

I have an offer code.

Go to Settings > Subscription > Redeem an offer code.

Using BibleChat

How do I change the language or the Bible wording?

Settings > your profile for the language BibleChat talks in, and Settings > Bible wording for the translation it quotes. Nothing needs to download. The app's menus follow your iPhone's language (Settings > BibleChat > Language).

A quoted verse looks different from my Bible.

BibleChat's AI quotes from memory and can get a word wrong. Tap the quote to see the whole passage, and report the answer if it's wrong.

How do I change how BibleChat answers?

Settings > Answer style: choose a tone and length, or let BibleChat learn from your conversations.

What does BibleChat know about me?

See and erase what it has learned in Settings > What BibleChat has learned, and its memories in Settings > See what BibleChat remembers. You can turn memory off.

I shared a conversation by mistake.

Open the conversation's share menu and remove the link. Shared links are public until you remove them.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Your conversations, journeys and settings sync through your iCloud, unless you chose to keep data on one device. Sign in with Apple in BibleChat to keep your account, gifts and invite rewards.

Can I hide BibleChat from others who use my phone?

Turn on discreet mode (Settings > Privacy and data): reminders show no text, the app switcher hides your screen, and you can choose a neutral app icon. You can also lock the app with Face ID or your passcode.

An answer was wrong or upsetting.

Use Report in the answer's menu. Read how our AI works.

Who can use BibleChat?

Anyone 13 and older (14 to 16 in some countries, and 18 in India; the app shows the age for yours). Teenagers get an experience with extra care: stricter AI guidance, no public conversation links, and analytics off unless they turn it on.

Your data

Export your data or delete your account in Settings > Privacy and data. See deleting your account and our Privacy Policy.
