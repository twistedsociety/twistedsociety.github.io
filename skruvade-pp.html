Charades

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Short version: the app collects no data about you. None.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party code in the app. We never see who plays, what you play or how often.

What stays on your phone

Player names, settings, your own decks and the cards you've already played are saved only on your device, so the game remembers them next time. Deleting the app deletes them.

Playing on several phones

When friends join with their own iPhone, the phones talk to each other directly over the local network, never through a server. The host's phone receives each guest's name, their moves and a random ID that lets a dropped phone rejoin. iOS asks for local network access only when you use this.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. It is hosted by GitHub, which, like any web host, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We don't access or use that data.

Children

The game is made for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles data, we'll update this page first.

Who we are

Twisted Society AB in Sweden makes the app and runs this website. Questions? Write to twistedsocietyab@gmail.com.
